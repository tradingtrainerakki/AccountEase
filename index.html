⚙️ GST Settings

👥 Party GST Details (GSTIN / State)

Grahak (Sundry Debtors) aur supplier (Sundry Creditors) ka GSTIN aur state yahan bharein. Isi se invoice me CGST+SGST ya IGST tay hota hai.

📑 GST Reports

🔁 GSTR-2B se Milan (khareed ka ITC)

GST portal se apna GSTR-2B (Excel .xlsx ya JSON) download karke yahan daalein. Ye aapki Purchase invoices (upar ke From / To ke bhitar) se supplier GSTIN aur invoice number par milaya jayega. Ek mahine ki 2B ho to From / To bhi usi mahine ke rakhein.

ℹ️ Is app me kya hai aur kya nahi

Hai: GST invoice (CGST+SGST / IGST), Sales / Purchase / Credit / Debit Note, Reverse Charge, ITC aur set-off ka hisaab, GSTR-1 / 3B jaise register, HSN summary, Tax Invoice print.
Nahi hai: e-invoice (IRN / QR), e-way bill, GST portal par seedha filing / JSON upload, TCS / TDS, Cess, export aur SEZ (zero-rated) supply, ITC blocked-credit (Sec. 17(5)) ki automatic jaanch.
Zaroor karein: return file karne se pehle apne CA se rates, ITC aur set-off mila lein. Rates aur niyam badalte rehte hain.
